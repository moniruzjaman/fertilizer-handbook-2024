🏛️ Mandatory DAE Advisory & BARC Statutory Disclaimer:
All fertilizer recommendations generated by this application are calculated strictly based on the Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council (BARC) Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2024 (FRG-2024) models and regional Agro-Ecological Zones (AEZ). Users are required to consult their local Sub-Assistant Agriculture Officer (SAAO) or Upazila Agriculture Office (Department of Agricultural Extension - DAE) and test plot soil with SRDI before bulk fertilizer application.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FRG 2024: Fertilizer Guide collects, handles, and protects user data in compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies.

2. Data We Collect

A. Location Data

We access your device's geographical coordinates (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) strictly in the foreground while the app is active. This data is used solely to:

  • Identify your Agro-Ecological Zone (AEZ 1 to 30) across Bangladesh.
  • Resolve reverse geocoded administrative addresses (Union, Upazila, District) for BARC regional guidance.
  • Fetch hyper-local agro-weather indices (spraying suitability, heat stress, rain forecast).
  • Enable GPS plot boundary mapping and area computation in decimals and bigha.

Location data is processed on-device and is never continuously tracked in the background.

B. Account Information

When you choose to sign in with Google via Android Jetpack Credential Manager, we store your Google account email and display name to associate your saved calculation records in Firebase Cloud Firestore. Guest mode is fully supported without sign-in.

3. Third-Party Integrations

  • Google Firebase Firestore: Cloud persistence for authenticated users' saved calculations.
  • Open-Meteo Weather API: Fetches agro-weather diagnostics via coordinate lookups without personal identification.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Translates coordinates into administrative Upazila and District names.

4. Data Deletion and User Rights

Users may delete saved calculations at any time through the in-app interface. To request the complete deletion of your account and cloud records, email mithun.hstu@gmail.com with the subject line "FRG 2024 Account Data Deletion Request".

5. Children's Privacy

This service is directed to farmers, agricultural students, and agronomy specialists and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
